# Pre-Build Validation Plan
### What we must confirm before writing a single line of app code

---

## The 3 Assumptions to Validate

### Assumption 1: Note templates alone would get someone to pay
- **Test:** Publish a free "Therapy Note Template Pack" (SOAP, DAP, BIRP in Google Doc form) to r/therapists
- **Post title:** "I made free structured note templates that cut my documentation time in half — here if you want them"
- **No product mention.** Just templates. Gauge interest.
- **Success metric:** 200+ downloads in first week, 20+ comments asking follow-up questions
- **What failure tells us:** Notes are not actually the pain we think they are

### Assumption 2: $99/month is a price they'll pay
- **Test:** Build a landing page (Framer or Vercel + Tailwind) describing the product, with pricing at $99/month
- **CTA:** "Join the waitlist — first 50 therapists get 3 months free"
- **Drive traffic:** Post in r/therapists + therapist Facebook groups
- **Success metric:** 50+ waitlist signups in 4 weeks without paid ads
- **What failure tells us:** Either price or product framing needs adjustment

### Assumption 3: Community can drive first customers without paid ads
- **Test:** Spend 4 weeks genuinely participating in r/therapists — answer questions about private practice setup, share useful resources, zero product pitches
- **After 4 weeks:** Single honest post — "I'm building a simple practice management tool for solo therapists — would love 10 beta testers in exchange for 6 months free"
- **Success metric:** 10+ DMs expressing interest in beta
- **What failure tells us:** Community acquisition won't work — need different channel

## Timeline
- Week 1-2: Build landing page + create template pack
- Week 1-4: Community presence building (r/therapists + FB groups)
- Week 2: Publish template pack, measure downloads
- Week 4: Post beta invite in community, measure DMs
- Week 5: 10 beta interviews — ask about pain, current tools, WTP
- Week 6: Decision — build or pivot

## Beta Interview Script (10 therapists)

**Opening:**
"Thanks for chatting. I'm building a tool for solo practice therapists. I'm not going to sell you anything — I just want to understand your day-to-day. Would it be okay if I asked you a few questions about how you run your practice?"

**Questions:**
1. How long have you been in solo practice? What pushed you to go out on your own?
2. Walk me through your week — outside of client sessions, where does your time go?
3. What tool do you use for scheduling? Notes? Billing? (Let them list — don't prompt)
4. What's the most annoying part of running your practice admin?
5. When did you last lose a session to a no-show? What did you do?
6. How long does it take you to write a note after a session?
7. Are you on SimplePractice or TherapyNotes? What do you like/hate about it?
8. If you could wave a magic wand and fix ONE thing about running your practice, what would it be?
9. What would you pay per month for something that eliminated most of your admin work?
10. If I built that — would you be a beta tester?

**Listen for:** Unprompted mentions of notes, no-shows, intake chaos, insurance superbills. These are the pain points worth building for.
