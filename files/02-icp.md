# Practice OS — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Primary User: Solo Therapist in Private Practice

### Demographics
- Licensed therapist (LCSW, LPC, MFT, psychologist)
- Solo practice — no employees, no group
- 2–10 years in private practice
- 15–25 active clients on caseload
- Private pay or hybrid (some insurance, mostly private pay)
- Running a practice that generates $80K–$250K/year in revenue

### Psychographic Profile
- Chose solo practice for autonomy, not to become a business manager
- Deeply values client relationships; sees dropout as personal failure
- Uncomfortable with sales/marketing language — "CRM" and "pipeline" feel wrong
- Time-scarce: sees 6–8 clients/day, little bandwidth for admin between sessions
- Technically capable (uses SimplePractice, Zoom, maybe an AI notes tool) but not a power user
- Already cobbling together 2–3 tools + spreadsheets to manage their practice

### Current Tool Stack (typical)
- **EHR**: SimplePractice or TherapyNotes (for notes, billing, scheduling)
- **AI Notes**: Mentalyc, Upheal, or Freed (added on top of EHR)
- **Business tracking**: Google Sheets, a paper notebook, or nothing
- **Client acquisition**: Psychology Today profile, word of mouth, maybe a personal website

### Their Biggest Frustrations (in their own words)
- "A client just stopped responding and I didn't realize until 3 weeks later"
- "I have no idea if I'm at capacity or not — I just go by feel"
- "I want to raise my rates but I don't know how it'll affect my income"
- "I spend Sunday nights doing notes and admin instead of recovering"
- "Psychology Today used to send me clients — it's dried up and I don't know what to do"

### Their Definition of Success
- Consistently full caseload (20–25 clients/week)
- Predictable monthly revenue
- No surprise dropouts
- Finishing admin within business hours
- Not thinking about the business when they're with clients

### Who Is NOT the ICP
- Group practice owners (need multi-clinician tooling, different price point)
- Therapists taking insurance-only (incentives are different, margin is thinner)
- New grads / pre-licensed therapists (not yet running a real business)
- Hospital/agency employed therapists (not running their own practice)
