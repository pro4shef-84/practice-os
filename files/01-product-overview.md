# Practice OS — Product Overview

## What It Is
A solo practice management tool for independent (solo) mental health therapists. Not another AI notes tool or EHR. Positioned as the **business layer** that sits above clinical documentation — the operating system for a one-person therapy business.

## The Core Problem
Solo therapists are simultaneously clinician, admin, and business owner. Every tool built for them solves the clinical side (notes, billing, scheduling) but leaves them completely blind on the business side:
- They don't know which clients are at dropout risk until they've already ghosted
- They have no visibility into their real capacity or utilization
- They track revenue with spreadsheets or gut feel
- No tool proactively surfaces "you're leaving money on the table"

## Tagline Options
1. "Notes tools save you time. This saves your practice."
2. "Built for therapists who are also the CEO."
3. "The operating system for your solo practice."

## Headline / Elevator Pitch
**"The business brain solo therapists never had."**

You became a therapist to help people, not to manage a small business. Practice OS handles the business layer — so your calendar stays full, your clients stay engaged, and you actually know how your practice is doing.

## Entry Wedge
**Caseload Intelligence** — Know which clients are at dropout risk before they ghost you. Understand your real capacity. See your effective hourly rate over time. All from one dashboard that speaks therapist, not spreadsheet.

## Positioned Against
Every other tool was built for the clinical side or for group practices. You get either a billing machine or a CRM that costs more than your rent and uses sales language that feels foreign and uncomfortable to a therapist. Practice OS is the first tool built for a solo therapist who is also a one-person business.

## Target Price
$49–79/month (comparable to SimplePractice tier, below group-practice CRM pricing of $200+/mo)
